# Community code of conduct

ArbSync is a maintainer-led project. Everyone participating in project issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful
environment, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be considerate, explain technical disagreements with evidence, accept constructive
feedback, and respect others' time and privacy. Critique ideas and code without personal
attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, doxxing, spam,
and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, ask the repository owner, [KassaSana](https://github.com/KassaSana),
for a private contact through a project issue containing only that request. Do not post
the incident, identities, or private evidence publicly. Share details only after a
private channel is arranged. The security reporting form is reserved for vulnerabilities.

The maintainer reviews reports in context, keeps information limited to what is needed
to investigate and act, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation,
or block participants. Serious threats or harassment may result in immediate removal.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is itself a violation. A participant
may request reconsideration through the same private channel and provide new context.

This small project has no independent moderation or appeals team. If a concern involves
the maintainer or project handling is unsuitable, participants may use GitHub's own
reporting tools for conduct on its platform.
